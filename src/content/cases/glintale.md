glintale is the thing I am building at the moment. The idea was mine. I am building it with a friend who is a developer, which makes two of us — the same shape as [ecoco](/case-studies/ecoco-mobile-app/), and I appear to have learned nothing about picking easier setups.

## Why this page is thin

Most case studies get written after the fact, when the outcome is known and the story can be arranged so the decisions look inevitable. I have written those. They are pleasant to read and not very useful, because the version that would actually help somebody is the one written while it is still uncertain.

So this page exists now, unfinished, and it will grow as the thing does. **If it fails, that gets written up here too**, which is the point of a site that claims to publish what things cost.

## What I know so far

That having the idea is the least valuable part, and I have to keep reminding myself of that. The idea took an afternoon. Everything since has been finding out whether anybody other than me has the problem, which is slower and much less flattering.

That building with one other person means every decision is a conversation, and that is mostly good. There is nowhere to hide a weak argument when there are two of you.

And that I am doing the thing I warn other people about: I am the user, which feels like the strongest possible evidence and is in fact the weakest. I am [running my own checks](/tools/) on it, and not always liking the answers.

## What I do not know

Whether the problem is painful enough that anybody would pay to have it solved. That is the only question that matters right now, and I do not have the answer yet.

There is a version of this page in six months that says it worked. There is another that says it did not. I would rather you saw this one first.
